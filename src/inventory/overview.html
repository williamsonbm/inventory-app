Click a row to change its stocking status, threshold or note. A blank threshold is "not set"; 0 means "reorder when none are left". LVL thresholds are set per depth, in linear feet, in Settings → LVL thresholds. Only an admin can rename an item. On Hand is what is here; below zero shows red. Incoming is what open POs still bring. Threshold, On Hand and Incoming are in linear feet (LF) for lumber, pieces for the rest. Reorder is Short below zero, Low at or below the threshold, otherwise OK; LVL is Low by its depth's linear feet, shown above its lengths.
