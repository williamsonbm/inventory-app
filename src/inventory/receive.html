Receive

Fill in only what arrived; a line left empty receives nothing. Pieces received fills in from packs × pack size + loose. To have the two checked, type the total from the Bill of Lading over it: if they do not add up, nothing saves. Split a line to enter one item that came in packs of different sizes. A pack size typed here is kept on this line; it becomes the item's size on file only if the item has none of that kind.

POs

Lumber is ordered in linear feet (LF), everything else in pieces; lumber is received in pieces. A pack size is the pieces in one pack, box or carton, if the PO names one. What is still to arrive on each open line shows as Incoming on the Overview. A line is never deleted: close one you no longer expect, with a reason, and re-open it if it comes after all. A PO is Finished when nothing more is expected on it.
